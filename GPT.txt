🧠 PART 1 — FIRST, WHAT EVEN IS PROLOG?

Prolog = PROgramming in LOGic.

Unlike C/Java/Python, where you usually tell the computer:

“Do these steps.”

In Prolog, you tell it:

“These facts are true, and these relationships are true. Now reason about them.”

For example:

male(john).
female(mary).
parent(john, michael).

You're saying:

John is male.
Mary is female.
John is the parent of Michael.

Then you can ask:

?- parent(john, michael).

Prolog answers:

true.

You aren't explicitly programming:

IF John is parent of Michael THEN return true.

The fact itself represents that knowledge.

🔥 PART 2 — THE THREE THINGS YOU MUST KNOW

Your entire practical basically revolves around:

1. Facts

Facts represent things that are known to be true.

male(john).
female(mary).
parent(john, michael).
Viva answer:

A fact is a statement that is directly known to be true in the Prolog knowledge base.

2. Rules

Rules define relationships using conditions.

Example:

father(X, Y) :-
    male(X),
    parent(X, Y).

Read this as:

X is the father of Y if X is male and X is a parent of Y.

The :- means roughly:

if

So:

father(X,Y) :- male(X), parent(X,Y).

means:

father(X,Y) is true if male(X) AND parent(X,Y) are true.

3. Queries

Queries are questions you ask Prolog.

?- father(john, michael).

Prolog searches its knowledge base.

If it can prove it:

true.

Otherwise:

false.

🔥 PART 7 — COMMA , VS SEMICOLON ;

Very likely logical viva question.

Comma = AND

    male(X),
    parent(X,Y).

means:

X is male AND X is parent of Y.

Both must succeed.

Semicolon = OR

Example:

    father(X,Y);
    mother(X,Y).

means:

X is father of Y OR X is mother of Y.

So:

,  → AND
;  → OR

🧠 PART 9 — = VS \= VS is

THIS is classic Prolog viva territory.

=

Unification.

X = john.

means:

Make X unify with john.

\=

Not unifiable.

X \= Y.

means they cannot be unified under the current bindings.

is

Used for arithmetic evaluation.

X is 2 + 3.

Then:

X = 5
Important:
X = 2 + 3.

does NOT calculate 5.

It makes X represent the term:

Whereas:

X is 2 + 3.

evaluates it.

Viva:

Q: Difference between = and is?

= performs unification, whereas is evaluates an arithmetic expression and assigns the resulting value.

🔁 PART 10 — WHY IS ANCESTOR RECURSIVE?

Your rule:

ancestor(X, Y) :-
    parent(X, Y).

ancestor(X, Y) :-
    parent(X, Z),
    ancestor(Z, Y).

There are two cases.

Base case
ancestor(X, Y) :-
    parent(X, Y).

If X directly parents Y:

X → Y

then X is an ancestor of Y.

Recursive case
ancestor(X, Y) :-
    parent(X, Z),
    ancestor(Z, Y).

Read:

X is an ancestor of Y if X is parent of Z and Z is an ancestor of Y.

Example:

John
 ↓
Michael
 ↓
David

Question:

ancestor(john, david).

Prolog can reason:

John is parent of Michael.
Michael is ancestor of David.
Therefore John is ancestor of David.

That's recursion.
